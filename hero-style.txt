Quiet, restrained editorial illustration for a technical blog. Hand-drawn ink line work with loose watercolour washes on warm off-white paper. A muted palette: mostly greys and warm paper tones, with one teal accent and one coral accent. Lots of empty space; the subject sits to one side and the rest of the frame is paper. No faces, no logos, no borders, no frames, no captions, no watermark. Calm, precise, a little wry.
